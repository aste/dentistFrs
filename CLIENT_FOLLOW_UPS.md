# Client follow-ups

## CPR handling

The current public booking form sends the full CPR number through Web3Forms to email. Do not describe this setup as verified or "bulletproof" until the clinic has reviewed processor agreements, international transfers, mailbox security, retention, access control, and incident handling.

Present these options to the client:

1. Remove CPR from the public booking request and collect it later through an approved patient system.
2. Integrate the form with the clinic's existing approved patient system.
3. Commission an EU-hosted secure intake portal with authentication, encryption, retention controls, audit logging, and appropriate processor agreements.

References:

- https://docs.web3forms.com/getting-started/faq
- https://www.datatilsynet.dk/regler-og-vejledning/behandlingssikkerhed/katalog-over-foranstaltninger/sikker-transmission

## Footer policies

Keep Cookiepolitik and Praktiske oplysninger og vilkår out of the release until the client confirms whether they are required and approves the final wording and presentation.
